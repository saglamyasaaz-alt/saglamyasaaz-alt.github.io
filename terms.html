ShortsBot Terms of Service

Operator: Sağlam Yaşa (YouTube channel @saglamyasa) · Effective date: 5 October 2026

1. The service

ShortsBot is a tool operated by Sağlam Yaşa. It publishes short videos that Sağlam Yaşa owns, which were first published on the Sağlam Yaşa YouTube channel, to the Sağlam Yaşa TikTok and YouTube accounts. The videos are converted to vertical 9:16 format and published with their original title and description.

2. Who may use it

Only accounts owned by Sağlam Yaşa are connected to ShortsBot. An account is connected only after its owner signs in with TikTok or Google and grants permission. The owner can revoke this permission at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Apps and services) or in Google account settings.

3. Content

  • Only original content owned by Sağlam Yaşa is published.
  • The account owner is responsible for the content and for following the TikTok Community Guidelines, the TikTok Terms of Service and the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • ShortsBot does not edit the meaning of the content and does not add watermarks or promotional material.

4. Data

How ShortsBot handles data is described in the ShortsBot Privacy Policy.

5. Availability and changes

ShortsBot is provided as is. Sağlam Yaşa may change or stop the service at any time. These terms may be updated; the effective date above shows the latest version.

6. Contact

Questions about these terms: saglamyasa.az@gmail.com
